## Lucide

The tab icons in `site/src/ui/tab-icons.ts` marked `lucide` use the paths of these Lucide 0.577.0 icons
(<https://lucide.dev>, <https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide>): `circle-play`, `gauge`, `grid-3x3`, `id-card`,
`cloud-sun`, `battery-charging`, `triangle-alert`, `brain`, `stamp`, `badge-check`, `folder`, `trophy`, `rocket`,
`gamepad-2` and `layers`, with circles, rectangles and lines written as paths, and `bot`, redrawn smaller without its
ears and with a plus beside it. They are drawn with 1.3 px strokes at 16 px and round caps and joins, as the
Terminal's 16 px glyphs are.
