- **BNB Agent Studio** has no logo of its own: its quickstart,
  <https://docs.bnbchain.org/developer-kit/bnbchain-studio/quickstart/> ("BNB Agent Studio Quickstart", read 08:45 CT),
  wears BNB Chain's. Its chip is `bnbchain.svg` with "Agent Studio" in words, and opens that page.
- **OpenFour** is "a new modular launch engine introduced by Four.Meme" (its documentation's README,
  <https://github.com/four-meme-community/openfour-docs>). Four.meme's wordmark on its site is white, for its dark
  page, so OpenFour's chip is Four.meme's square mark with "OpenFour" in words, and opens <https://four.meme/>.
- **BNB Chain mainnet** in the hub's diagram is `bnbchain.svg` with "Mainnet" in words.
